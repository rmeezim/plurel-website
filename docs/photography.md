# Plurel: the photography the interior pages are waiting for

**The rule is the hero film's** (`docs/hero-video.md`): real photographs
of real work, made by real people. No stock, nothing generated, no
staged "team" shots with models. The site is about proof, and a stock or
generated picture is the first thing a buyer learns to discount.

Until the photographs exist, every page carries its point in drawings
(each service page opens on its own signature, `components/signatures.tsx`)
and no page shows an empty picture box. When a set below is shot, it goes
into the page as a full-bleed band or beside the head, in place of
nothing that is already there.

## What to shoot, page by page

| Page | Shot | Why it earns its place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About | The team at work: a working session around one brief, the Ledger read on a screen, hands on real material | Shows the one accountable team the site keeps naming |
| Method | The Diagnostic being made: the six parts on a table or wall, a strategist marking the read | Makes the first step tangible |
| Video & Photo | Stills from a real shoot: the widest frame with the crop marks taped on the monitor, a creator being directed | Proof of the "lit for the widest, composed for the narrowest" claim |
| UGC & Creators | A creator filming a hook to a brief (with a signed release), the brief beside them | Shows the brief-first way creators work with Plurel |
| Branding | A brand system laid out: type specimens, colour chips, the templates in use on real pieces | The specimen in the head, made physical |
| Website Design | A page on a phone in a buyer's hand, in daylight | The page as a buyer meets it |
| Content Marketing | The recorded conversation: two people, one microphone, the subject expert talking | The source every piece is cut from |
| PR & Reputation | A founder in a podcast studio or on a stage, with the outlet's permission | Coverage as it is made |

## How to shoot it

- Daylight or practical light, never a seamless backdrop. Real rooms,
  real screens, real work on them (with the client's permission).
- Graded toward the site: warm reds kept, cool shadows, no orange-teal
  look. Graphite and red should sit next to it without a seam.
- People in frame are the team or have signed a release; client work
  appears only with the client's permission.
- Delivered at 2400px on the long side, 3:2 and 4:5 crops of every
  frame, so the same picture can sit beside a head or in a phone layout.
